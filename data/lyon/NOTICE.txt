# Notice on the Lyon case-study data

This directory contains processed data derived from third-party sources, together with
saved experimental outputs produced by the authors of this repository.

## OpenStreetMap

Parts of the graph (the OSM-derived nodes and walking/free edges) are derived from
OpenStreetMap data.

(ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Derived data distributed here remains subject to the ODbL, including its attribution and
share-alike obligations. The software licence of this repository does not apply to
this data.

## GTFS transit data

The transit lines, line-stop sequences and GTFS-prefixed stop nodes (`lines.csv`,
`line_stops.csv`, `nodes_gtfs_stops.csv`) were derived from a GTFS feed for the Lyon
region. The authors' archive does not record the feed provider, URL, version or retrieval
date, and the licence of the original feed has not been verified.

Before reusing or redistributing these files, verify the terms of the original feed.
Do not assume that a currently available GTFS feed for Lyon is the one used in this study.

## Provenance limitations

The exact OSM extraction date and query extent are not preserved. The processed graph and
the saved runs (`case_study_raw_runs.jsonl`) are sufficient to reproduce the reported
analysis, but the graph cannot be rebuilt from the original external sources.

## Authors' outputs

`case_study_raw_runs.jsonl` and `case_study_indicators.jsonl` are results generated by
the authors with the code in this repository, from the processed graph described above.
